Three Group-level plays · what to move first

What we found

Knix Group DMARC sits at p=none across knix.com, wearmntd.ca, and wearmntd.com — while SAXX runs p=reject; pct=100. Gmail's Feb 2024 bulk-sender guidance pushes toward p=quarantine minimum for senders at Klaviyo scale.

What we'd do

Move Knix Group to p=quarantine across all three sender domains, then earn BIMI. One config change, three brands lifted.

What it protects

Inbox placement + brand-mark trust across the whole portfolio at once.

What we found

All three brands sit at Segmentation 2/5 despite Klaviyo capability — SAXX runs 1.3% subject personalization, MNTD sends its XXXL launch list-wide with size on file, Knix serves English-only email despite en-CA + en-US hreflang on the storefront.

What we'd do

Klaviyo profile properties for size, gender, and language, fed into templates via merge tags and behavioural segment splits.

What it unlocks

The highest-leverage dimension moves 2 → 3+ across the group.

What we found

Offer strategy inconsistent across the group. SAXX 15–40% with bundle framing (differentiated). MNTD single 15% code with SMS-vs-email conflict (20% on SMS, 15% on email — same code). Knix 10–60% deep-ladder (60% risks margin erosion).

What we'd do

Discount-ladder standard + code-consistency audit across email, SMS, and the storefront popup. Bundle-framed offers as the default lever, not blanket percentage cuts.

What it unlocks

Margin protection at Knix depth · offer clarity at MNTD level.

The lift isn't at the brand — it's at the Group.

DMARC, segmentation, and offer consistency are portfolio plays. Fix once at the Knix Group configuration layer and all three brands benefit. MNTD's own audit already earns the strongest Lifecycle score of the three (4/5, inside-out) — the ceiling on its scoring is set by policy it inherits, not by anything MNTD is doing wrong.
